# Participant brief

Thank you for trying this synthetic filing-review exercise. Participation is
voluntary; you may stop at any time. Use a pseudonym. Do not enter real customer,
policyholder, insurer, or confidential information.

## What Lumen does

Lumen is a decision-support prototype for a fictional insurance rate revision. It
recomputes selected figures, shows source evidence, and presents AI-generated
review notes. It does not decide whether a filing is legal, accurate, or ready to
submit. The Lumen-assisted portion uses a saved demonstration review and makes
no new AI request; it tests the interface and evidence workflow, not a fresh
model response.

## Your task in each method

Review the same synthetic filing and list concerns you would raise to a filing
manager. For each concern, record:

1. Up to four things that look wrong or need checking, with the affected
   segment or document.
2. For each, note which source file or application view led you to it.
3. For one concern you choose, use the final minute to check its source, explain
   what the source supports, and state a sensible analyst next step.

In the **manual** portion, use only the three supplied files. A calculator or
spreadsheet is fine; do not use AI or Lumen.

In the **Lumen-assisted** portion, open the built-in **Flawed** review in Lumen.
Repeat the task, then choose one concern and open its cited evidence. Say what the source
shows and what you would do next. Treat model notes as leads to verify, not as
facts or legal conclusions.

There is a four-minute target for listing concerns and one additional minute for
the evidence/action check in each method. Work at your normal pace. If you are
not finished, say so; the observer will record the actual time and incomplete
tasks rather than guess.

We are evaluating how understandable and useful the workflow is, not testing you.
Please say what is confusing as it occurs. There are no expected comments or
preferred answers.
